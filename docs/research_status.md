# Research status and scientific boundaries

Updated 21 October 2026.

Flood Sentinel is rebuilding its scientific evidence. Existing completion declarations, labels, predictions and headline metrics do not establish a valid warning study. Prior source substitutions, unsupported target definitions and evaluation defects prevent those results from serving as new flood-performance evidence. This is a limitation of the evidence, not an allegation about any person's intent.

A new study must establish genuine source bytes and versioned extraction, compatible units and catchment identities, real or explicitly assumed availability, complete future monitoring for negative labels, and interval-aware future-onset truth. It must retain every eligible issue and all relevant non-event exposure. An empty event catalog is insufficient evidence of no flood. Missing or ambiguous truth remains unknown.

High-flow discharge quantiles and official flood thresholds answer distinct questions. Fixed retrospective flow thresholds do not establish official flood impacts or inundation. Current threshold metadata alone do not establish historic datum/rating/threshold validity. Final reanalysis products are retrospective; physical observation-window closure does not establish that an input was published at issue time.

Training and evaluation must separate sites, connected river networks, time periods and calibration roles according to the declared task. Train-only fitting uses unique permitted source records. Seeds represent training variability conditional on the corpus, not additional independent rivers or floods. Unsupported subgroup metrics remain undefined. Non-significance does not establish equivalence.

Comparators need common information opportunity and genuine optimization. Last-value persistence, rate-of-change and a no-alert rule are different policies. Recursive statistical state must not reset at every input window if a continuous detector is claimed. A station-ID encoding is not a verified physical catchment attribute.

Any future paper will distinguish actual observational findings, disclosed constructed software fixtures, analytical capacity models and untested proposals. Benefits, degradation, inconclusive findings and source infeasibility may all be valid outcomes. A broken protocol is an invalid-method finding rather than evidence that a forecasting hypothesis succeeds or fails.

There is no established operational service, validated Nepal deployment, independent scientific certification, journal acceptance or submission-ready result. Public research artifacts and lawful reproduction instructions will be released only when they support the stated claims. Author responsibility, actual assistance and material scientific limitations will be disclosed truthfully under the applicable venue rules.
